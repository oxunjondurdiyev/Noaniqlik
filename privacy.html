Privacy policy

Document version: 2026-11-21 · Terms of use

This document explains which personal data the UncertaintyCalc platform processes, for what purpose, where and for how long. The owner of the platform and the data operator is Oxunjon Durdiyev.

1. What data is collected

  • When an organisation registers: taxpayer number (INN), phone number, first and last name, login, number of employees using the platform.
  • When the organisation administrator adds an employee: the employee's name, phone number, position and login.
  • For sign-in: a technical address derived from the login and a password hash. The password is not stored in clear text and the platform owner cannot see it.
  • Service data: plan, payment and activation status, registration date, the version of the consent document and the time consent was given.

2. Data that is not sent to the server

  • Measurement data — observations, the uncertainty budget, results, report details, the register of standards and quality control charts — is stored only in your browser and is not sent to the server.
  • The site does not use cookies, advertising or external analytics services. Only settings (language, theme) and the sign-in session are kept in browser storage.
  • The AI assistant is off by default. If an organisation turns it on and uses its own key, the request is sent to the AI service chosen by the user; the content of a request is limited to a predefined allow-list, and document text is sent only with the user's separate consent.

3. Why it is needed

  • Creating an account and signing in.
  • Contacting the organisation about payment and activation, preparing an invoice.
  • Letting the organisation administrator manage access for its employees.
  • The data is not used for advertising and is not sold to third parties.

4. Where it is stored

  • Registration and organisation data is stored in Google Firebase services (Firestore and Firebase Authentication) — on Google Cloud servers, outside the territory of the Republic of Uzbekistan.
  • The data can be seen by the organisation administrator, active employees of that organisation and the platform owner (super-admin). An employee's data is visible to the employee, the administrator of their organisation and the platform owner.

5. How long it is kept

  • The data is kept for as long as the organisation's account exists. When the account is deleted, the organisation's and employees' data is deleted as well.
  • An exact retention period for inactive or unpaid accounts has not been set yet; once set, it will be published on this page.

6. Your rights and deletion requests

  • You may ask to see, correct or delete the information about you, and you may withdraw your consent.
  • The request is sent through the contact channels below and should state the organisation's taxpayer number and the login. Once the requester is verified, the data is corrected or deleted.
  • Withdrawing consent means deleting the account: the account cannot be used without the registration data.

7. Changes to this document

  • The document version is shown at the top of the page. The version you agreed to at registration and the time of consent are stored with your account.
