Professional Measurement Uncertainty Calculator

Fully compliant with ISO/IEC 17025:2017 and GUM:2008 — the global gold standards for calibration laboratories.

Core formula (GUM 5.1):

uA = s(x) / √n
uB = a / √3
uc = √[ uA² + Σ(ci·uBi)² ]
U = k · uc   (k=2 → 95%)

Everything a Metrologist Needs

Type A Analysis

Automatic statistical evaluation: mean, standard deviation, and standard uncertainty of the mean.

Type B Distributions

Four distributions from GUM 4.3: rectangular, normal, triangular, U-shape.

Combined Uncertainty

Law of propagation with sensitivity coefficients. Welch-Satterthwaite effective degrees of freedom.

ISO/IEC 17025 Report

Printable uncertainty budget table — ready to attach to calibration certificates.

Three Languages

Full interface in Uzbek, Russian and English. Language preference saved automatically.
